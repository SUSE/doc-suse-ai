[#ai-factory-managing-blueprints-modify]
== How do I modify blueprints?

To modify a blueprint, follow these steps:

. From the main left panel, click menu:SUSE AI Factory[Blueprints].
. From the list of existing blueprints, select the one to modify, click its three-dot menu and select btn:[Edit].
. On the `Basic Info` screen, update the version number and description.
. On the `Select Apps` screen, update the list of blueprint's applications by adding new applications or removing existing ones.
Confirm with btn:[Next].
. On the `Configuration` screen, review and optionally update the blueprint applications' {helm} charts.
. On the `Review & Create` screen, verify that the entered information is correct and confirm with btn:[Save as New Version].

[#ai-factory-managing-blueprints-copy]
== How do I base a blueprint on an existing one?

To base a blueprint on an existing one, follow these steps:

. From the main left panel, click menu:SUSE AI Factory[Blueprints].
. From the list of existing blueprints, select the one to clone, specify its version, click its three-dot menu and select btn:[Copy].
. On the `Basic Info` screen, you can modify the suggested name, version and description.
. On the `Select Apps` screen, update the list of blueprint's applications by adding new applications or removing existing ones.
Confirm with btn:[Next].
. On the `Configuration` screen, review and optionally update the blueprint's deployment {helm} chart.
. On the `Review & Create` screen, verify that the entered information is correct and confirm with btn:[Create].

[#ai-factory-managing-blueprints-deprecate]
== How do I deprecate blueprints?

To deprecate a blueprint and hide it from the list of blueprints, follow these steps:

. From the main left panel, click menu:SUSE AI Factory[Blueprints].
. From the list of existing blueprints, select the one to deprecate.
. Specify the version to deprecate, click its three-dot menu and select btn:[Deprecate].
. Confirm with btn:[Deprecate].

[#ai-factory-managing-blueprints-undeprecate]
== How do I restore deprecated blueprints?

To restore a deprecated blueprint and make it appear in the list of blueprints, follow these steps:

. From the main left panel, click menu:SUSE AI Factory[Blueprints].
. A deprecated blueprint is hidden from the list of blueprints.
To make it appear again, activate the btn:[Show deprecated] check box.
. Select the blueprint to restore and specify its deprecated version.
. Click the blueprint's three-dot menu and select btn:[Undeprecate].
. Confirm with btn:[Undeprecate].

[#ai-factory-managing-blueprints-delete]
== How do I delete blueprints?

To delete a blueprint, follow these steps:

. From the main left panel, click menu:SUSE AI Factory[Blueprints].
. From the list of existing blueprints, select the one to delete and specify the version to delete.
. Click the blueprint's three-dot menu and select btn:[Delete].
Confirm with btn:[Delete].

[#ai-factory-managing-blueprints-multiple-envs]
== How do I manage environment-specific configurations with blueprints?

Consider the following use-case:
You have a {litellm} blueprint and want to install it on a development and production cluster using different configurations.
Due to reduced hardware resources on the development cluster, you need different LLM models, {ingress} values or GPU resource settings.
For clarity, the blueprints are named `blueprint-litellm-dev` and `blueprint-litellm-prod`.
To achieve this task with {productname}, follow these steps:

. Use a prebuilt blueprint or create a custom blueprint as described in xref:ai-factory-managing-blueprints-create[].
This blueprint will be deployed in production, therefore update its name to `blueprint-litellm-prod`.
. Create a new version of the blueprint with the desired configurations as described in xref:ai-factory-managing-blueprints-modify[].
. Clone the blueprint as described in xref:ai-factory-managing-blueprints-copy[] and name it `blueprint-litellm-dev`.
. Deploy the blueprints as described in xref:ai-factory-managing-blueprints-install[]. Specify the correct version for each deployment.
.. blueprint `blueprint-litellm-prod` on the production cluster
.. blueprint `blueprint-litellm-dev` on the development cluster

[TIP]
====
If you need to apply a new version of a blueprint during future maintenance, go to menu:SUSE AI Factory[Workloads] and click the corresponding btn:[Upgrade] button.
====
