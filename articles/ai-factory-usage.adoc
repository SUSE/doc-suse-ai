{productname} is a {ranchera} UI extension and a {kube} operator that allows you to discover AI applications and compose them into immutable, version-controlled stacks called Blueprints.

WHY?::
Use {productname} to simplify the management and deployment of AI applications on your {kube} cluster.

EFFORT::
Learn the core features of {productname} in less than 30 minutes.

GOAL::
By following the described procedures, you will be able to discover applications or create your first Blueprint.
